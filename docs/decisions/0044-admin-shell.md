# 0044: The admin shell: sections in the sidebar, "Needs you" for those who can act, the log naming its people, and an info tone

- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Status:** Decided; applied in K4.6 (#84)
- **Context:** K4.6 builds the admin's frame, its Dashboard and its Activity page to VM Website's `design/site/AdminDashboard.dc.html` and `AdminActivity.dc.html` in the admin's look (K4 contract §4 item 6; decisions 0040 and 0042). Building them found:
  - The boards' sidebar lists sections, not pages: "People and access", never People, Roles and Activity. `AdminBlog.dc.html` shows a section's pages indented under it while one of them is open; `AdminActivity.dc.html`, drawn before, marks the section itself.
  - The Activity board names who did each thing ("Big Haas, Owner") and to whom ("Changed J Tone's role"), filters by kind (Sign-in, Accounts and roles, Moderation, Settings), and says how long entries are kept. The staff's log (`GET /api/kernel/admin/audit`) answered ids, one action at a time, and nothing about the keep.
  - "Needs you" is for those who can act on each item (contract §2, addition 9). Acting on a failed job is retrying it, under `kernel.jobs.manage`, which the contract had arrive with System (K4.8).
  - The boards draw a third tone, info, in volt (`#36B4FF`): the spotlight item, the New members pill, the stat cards' links. The kernel's tones were success, warning and danger; change 15 left info for the dashboard.
  - The session answers role ids and no name, and nothing yet lets a person give one.
- **Decision:**
  1. **The sidebar is sections.** The top group's entries (Dashboard, and module entries with no section) are links of their own. Each other section is one link, with its icon, to its first page the person may open; while one of its pages is open, its pages show under it, the open one current (`AdminBlog.dc.html`'s drawing). A link is current only at its own address when another page sits under it (Site, then Features). People and access takes the lock, Settings the gear.
  2. **The top bar:** the breadcrumb (Admin, the section, the page; a page may name itself, "Activity log"), Search or jump to (Ctrl K or ⌘ K anywhere in the admin: a dialog with a combobox over the pages the person can see, by name or section), the notices (the person's own, `GET /api/kernel/notifications`, from the bell), View site. Under 960px the sidebar folds behind Menu, which opens it in place under the bar and closes on the next page.
  3. **`AdminPage`** is the page header for every admin page, a module's too (`@/kernel`): title, one line, actions; it gives the head's title and the breadcrumb's last part.
  4. **`GET /api/kernel/admin/attention`**: `{items: [{key, tone, what, why, action: {label, to}}]}`, the worst first. The kernel's items: `kernel.jobs.dead` (danger) for those holding both `kernel.settings.manage` (System is read with it) and `kernel.jobs.manage`; `kernel.site.description_missing` (warning) and `kernel.site.card_missing` (info) for those holding `kernel.settings.manage`. Staff with a second factor only, as every admin route.
  5. **`kernel.jobs.manage` lands now** (migration `kernel_0011`, admin by default), a PR before System's retry and discard use it, so the failed-jobs item's gate is final.
  6. **The staff's log names its people and its keep, and takes several actions:** the answer gains `people` (each actor and subject on the page who still has an account, by id: `{name, email, roles: [{id, name}]}`) and `kept_days`; `action` may be given up to 50 times, any of them matching. An entry with no actor is the kernel's own when it holds no address or browser ("MechFrame, System", as the board writes it), else someone not signed in.
  7. **An info tone:** required tokens `color.info` and `color.info-tint`. Vibrant Mindz's are volt and volt at 12%; Machined Light's `info-500` is 3.6:1 as words on its canvas, so it takes `info-700` `#2864B8`, derived on its hue as the other signals were (4.9:1 on the canvas and its tint). `Pill` and `Attention` take `info`; the kit gains `StatCard`, the dashboard's card.
  8. **Left off, not faked:** the greeting's name and the person's name in the sidebar (the session has none, and nothing sets one), shown as the email; role names in the sidebar's person card come from the role ids (Owner, Admin) until the roles read (K4.7); the Activity board's search and Export CSV (no route); its Content and Money kinds (modules' actions, which show under All with their module's name until modules declare kinds); the Dashboard's launch checklist and figures (a site's and its modules', through `admin.dashboard.widget`).
- **Consequences:** a module's admin pages use `AdminPage` and its sections' icons come from `adminSections`; a module's dashboard card is a `StatCard` in its `admin.dashboard.widget` part; its own "Needs you" items wait for the dashboard cards hook (decision 0018). A theme defines two more tokens.
