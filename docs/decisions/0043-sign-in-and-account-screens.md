# 0043: The sign-in and account screens: a frame of their own, the site's look in the kit, and the account's reads

- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Status:** Decided; applied in K4.5 (#83)
- **Context:** K4.5 builds the kernel's sign-in, sign-up, landing and account screens to VM Website's `design/site/Auth.dc.html` and `Account.dc.html` (K4 contract §4 item 5, decision 0042). Building them found:
  - The Auth board draws its own frame: the site's name and "Back to the site" above, the form beside the site's mark, one line below. SPEC 8 put the kernel's public pages in the site frame, with its menus.
  - The boards' controls are the site's size, not the admin's: 54px buttons and fields on sign-in, 44px round buttons on the account, larger cards with display titles (shell-slots.md, findings 3 to 5).
  - The Account board shows whether the authenticator app is on and since when, and how many recovery codes are left. No route answered either.
  - The device list's routes said `204` and nothing more: not what a foreign id answers, nor what ending this device's own session does.
  - Writes from the end-to-end tests' browser (`http://localhost:8765`) were refused as cross-site, since the e2e server's site was `https://mechframe.test`, and a passkey's origin couldn't match it either.
- **Decision:**
  1. **The sign-in frame.** The kernel's public pages (signing in and up, forgot password, the seven landings from mail) sit in a frame of their own, as the Auth board draws it: no site menus, the form column beside the site's `logo` when it has one. Member pages stay in the site frame. SPEC 8 changes.
  2. **The site's look is the kit's, not a second kit.** The site's frames carry `.mf-site-look`: buttons fully round and 44px, cards padded 28px at a 22px radius under a display title, pills with the tone in the dot. `Button` gains `size="large"` (54px) and `variant="strong"` (the passkey's); `Field` gains `size="large"` (54px at a 14px radius, the hint below) and `aside` (a link beside the label, never inside it); `Card` gains `description`; the icons gain `key` and `mail`. Sizes are fixed in code at the boards' values, as the admin's are.
  3. **`GET /api/auth/security`** (new): `{password, totp: {since} | null, recovery_codes_left}`. A TOTP set up but not confirmed is none.
  4. **The device routes, written down** (auth-api.md): the list is the sessions that still count, latest seen first, `location` null until the site can place a browser. Ending this device's own session is signing out: the cookie is cleared, even when the request was given a new token after a role change. Any other id, another account's included, answers 404 `auth.session_not_found`. None asks for a step-up: ending a session only takes access away.
  5. **The step-up dialog** is one for every write refused together. Closing it is a no: each write fails with its `auth.step_up_required`, which a screen says nothing about. It offers a passkey, the password or the app's code, as the account has them, and takes the look of the frame it opened over.
  6. **Left off, not faked:** sign-up's name field and its news box (the kernel's sign-up takes `{email, password}`); the password's "Last changed" (nothing records it); "Delete account" (K2 step 11); the board's own words ("Join the vibe", "Vibrify ya mind"), where the kernel says plain ones.
  7. **A QR code for the authenticator app's key** (the owner, 2026-11-19: "Yes, add a QR code"). The kit's `QrCode` draws uqr's module grid (0.1.3: MIT, no dependencies, about 80 KB unpacked) as one SVG path, error correction M with the standard's 4-module quiet zone. A camera reads dark on light, so it takes the system's light `Canvas` and `CanvasText`, never a theme's colours, and forced colours leave it alone. jsQR (1.4.0, Apache-2.0, a dev dependency only) reads it back in the tests: in Vitest from the drawn path, and in Playwright from what Chromium draws in each theme, dark on light only.
  8. **The e2e server's site is the address the browser opens**, `http://localhost:<port>`, with DEBUG on (an http or local site address is refused otherwise) and rate limits off (the tests sign in side by side from one address; the contract suite checks each limit).
- **Consequences:** a site's own sign-in look comes from its theme and its `logo`; its words for these screens wait for a way to reword kernel screens. A module's page in the site frame gets the site's button and card sizes. The account page reads three routes (`/security`, `/passkeys`, `/sessions`) and the session.
