# 0006: The new Vibrant Mindz site is the first site

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Status:** Locked
- **Context:** The owner chose the new Vibrant Mindz site (Q10). It needs custom apparel, embeds, beat and composition licensing, a Discord-style community and blog comments.
- **Decision:** The site maps to 17 modules plus 2 new kernel services (docs/plans/vibrant-mindz-module-plan.md). It launches once payments, shop and downloads work, and its build doubles as the spec's validation, since blog, chat and payments are all on it.
- **Alternatives:** A separate validation exercise before any real site.
- **Consequences:** Build order: kernel and content modules, comments and reactions, community, commerce (launch), licensing and the audio player, then print on demand.
- **See:** docs/plans/vibrant-mindz-module-plan.md
